## Brand & Style
The brand personality reflects German engineering precision, architectural restraint, and bespoke concierge reliability. Catering to affluent homeowners, interior architects, and estate managers across Moscow and prestigious suburban residences (Rublyovka, Novorizhskoye Shosse), the visual identity eliminates all tropes of low-cost domestic maintenance—rejecting urgent discount badges, garish countdowns, and harsh neon accents. 

Instead, the UI draws directly from Gaggenau’s physical design ethos: massive tinted obsidian glass, bead-blasted aluminum, precision knurled rotary dials, and hairline metallic seams. The atmosphere is quiet, confident, and meticulously organized. Content density is controlled with generous negative space, crisp architectural typography, tabular data alignments, and deep layered darks. Micro-interactions are deliberate and dampened, conveying mechanical heft and flawless craftsmanship.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in an ultra-deep obsidian base `#0c0e12` that prevents eye fatigue while conveying monolithic physical substance. Layering occurs through stepped neutral darks: `#14171d` for elevated panels and `#1c2029` for interactive containers and floating elements. 

The primary chromatic accent is a muted, warm metallic champagne brass (`#c5a059` and `#d4b483`), used strictly for primary conversions, status indicators, and micro-accents. Vivid yellows, aggressive reds, and mass-market blue tones are forbidden. Structural hierarchy relies on silvered cool neutrals: `#f4f5f7` for razor-sharp typography, `#a3a8b5` for technical descriptions, and `#686e7d` for legal microcopy. Hairline borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`) outline glass panels with architectural precision, echoing milled metal seams.

## Typography
Typographic expression balances architectural authority with technical precision. Primary headlines leverage `Plus Jakarta Sans` set at medium to semi-bold weights with tight negative tracking, conveying understated poise. Running body text uses `Inter` for clarity across both Russian and English localizations, naturally handling Russian ascender traits and English line compression.

`Space Grotesk` is designated for high-precision technical data, metrics, SLA numerical counters, and eyebrow caps labels (`label-caps`). Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced for diagnostic metrics, response timers, and pricing disclosures to guarantee alignment across data grids.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a strict, disciplined geometric framework built on an 8px base rhythm. Outer layouts utilize a 12-column grid for standard layouts, adapting into an intentional 5-column architectural layout across wide desktop screens (`> 1280px`) to display the 5 core Gaggenau appliance categories without artificial wrap.

On mobile devices, layout shifts to a single column with a mandatory reserved clearance of `5.5rem` (`88px`) at the screen bottom to house the sticky conversion bar without occluding content or legal disclaimers. Section vertical paddings alternate between `space-2xl` and `space-3xl`, maintaining structural breathability that reflects high-end gallery design rather than commercial volume density.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is realized through physical material layers rather than blurred drop shadows. The design system rejects heavy black drop shadows in favor of stacked tonal tiers, glass refraction, and hairline boundary illumination:

1. **Surface 0 (Base Canvas):** Pure solid `#0c0e12`, absorbing background noise.
2. **Surface 1 (Structural Modules & Cards):** `#14171d` with an all-around `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Hover states induce an inner specular glow of `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)` across the perimeter.
3. **Surface 2 (Glassmorphic Elements):** `rgba(20, 23, 29, 0.72)` coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle directional top-edge gradient border simulating an illuminated metal chamfer (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.14) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.02) 100%)`).
4. **Surface 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Nav):** `#1c2029` set atop an obsidian scrim (`rgba(12, 14, 18, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), accented by a diffuse champagne ambient aura (`box-shadow: 0 24px 64px -16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`).

## Shapes
In harmony with German architectural modernism and the geometric minimalism of luxury built-in appliances, the shape language uses soft, engineered radiuses (Level 1). Functional surfaces, diagnostic cards, input fields, and modal frames share a consistent `0.25rem` (`4px`) or `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius, avoiding soft "bubble" rounding.

Pill-shaped geometry (`roundedness: 3` / `9999px`) is reserved exclusively for micro status pills, SLA trust chips, and the language selector toggle (`RU | EN`), creating an intentional visual distinction between foundational structural surfaces and compact interactive micro-components.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Metallic CTA ("Оставить заявку"):** Base `#c5a059` gradient tinted to `#d4b483` with text in `#0c0e12` (semibold). Height: `48px` (desktop), `44px` (mobile). Radius: `4px`. Micro-hover lifts with a subtle warm specular glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`). Active state applies a `0.98` scale down.
- **Secondary Brushed Outline ("Позвонить"):** Surface transparent, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`, text `#f4f5f7`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` and border transitions to `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`.
- **Ghost Actions:** Text `#a3a8b5`, no background or border, transitioning to `#f4f5f7` on interaction.

### Header Navigation
- Height `72px`, fixed with glassmorphism (`rgba(12, 14, 18, 0.85)` + `12px` blur). Border bottom: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- Left: Architectural working identity placeholder ("Официальный сервис Gaggenau") in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 14px uppercase with subtle letter-spacing.
- Center/Right: Tabular direct dispatch phone link in `Space Grotesk`, segmented pill language switch (`RU | EN`) with a sliding dark brass active tab, and condensed Primary CTA button.

### Appliance Cards (5 Categories)
- Vertical container with an aspect ratio tuned for machinery silhouettes (Ovens, Dishwashers, Refrigeration, Cooktops, Coffee Systems).
- Surface `#14171d` with hairline outline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. 
- Header includes appliance category designation in `Space Grotesk` uppercase, followed by model series applicability (e.g., "Series 200 / 400").
- Bottom action reveals a subdued arrow icon shifting `+4px` on hover with hairline accent illumination in `#c5a059`.

### SLA & Trust Badges
- Compact pill chips (`height: 28px`, `border-radius: 9999px`) on `rgba(28, 32, 41, 0.85)` background.
- Left edge features a `6px` radial breathing indicator (pulsing green `#4ade80` for live master dispatch availability; warm champagne `#c5a059` for parts authenticity and 12-month warranty guarantees).
- Typography set in `Space Grotesk` 11px uppercase with `0.08em` tracking.

### Form Inputs & Checkbox
- Minimalist dark fields: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, bottom border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`. Radius `4px 4px 0 0`.
- Text `#f4f5f7`, placeholder `#686e7d`. Focus state illuminates bottom border in solid `#c5a059` with zero outline blur.
- Consent Checkbox: Bespoke `18x18px` square container with `2px` radius. Unchecked: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Checked: Background `#c5a059`, checkmark `#0c0e12`. Accompanying legal string set in `caption-legal` (`#686e7d`).

### Promo Banner ("Сезонная диагностика")
- Muted obsidian-to-charcoal linear gradient frame (`135deg, #1c2029 0%, #14171d 100%`) with an ultra-thin champagne perimeter border `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`.
- Replaces mass-market discount stars with a clean tabular SLA diagnostic checklist. Legal disclaimer placed at base in `#686e7d` regarding conditional diagnostic deductions on service completion.

### Mobile Sticky Bar
- Fixed to mobile viewport bottom (`z-index: 100`). Padding: `12px 16px`, background `rgba(12, 14, 18, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border top: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Dual layout: 1:1 ratio between Secondary "Позвонить" button (with phone icon) and Primary metallic "Заявка" button.
